2. Website, development & consulting services

For recurring or continuing services, cancellation will normally take effect at the end of the current paid service period unless the applicable agreement states otherwise. If you believe you have made a duplicate payment or an incorrect payment to ALPHAONE TECHNOLOGIES SDN BHD, contact us promptly with the transaction details. We will review the payment and, where appropriate, arrange a refund to the original payment method or another method agreed with you.

Email alphaonetech00@proton.me with your name, company name, invoice or reference number, payment date, amount and reason for the request. We may ask for supporting information before deciding the request.

7. Processing

Where a refund is approved, we will normally process it within a reasonable period and use the original payment method where practicable. The time for funds to appear may depend on the bank or payment provider.

This policy does not remove or limit any consumer or other legal rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. If a mandatory legal right to a refund or cancellation applies, that right will prevail.
