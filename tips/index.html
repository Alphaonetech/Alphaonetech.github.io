01ABOUT TIPS

OUR BUSINESS

Practical solutions for a connected journey.

TIPS GLOBAL SOLUTION SDN. BHD. is a Malaysian private limited company incorporated on 8 September 2015. The company was previously named TIPS TRAVEL SDN. BHD., with the name changed in July 2023.

Its registered business nature covers mobile telecommunication services to inbound travellers, event management and payment solutions — bringing travel, experiences and transaction support together.
